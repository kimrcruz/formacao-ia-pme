Onboarding screen: six directions

The first screen a participant sees after scanning the QR code at the workshop (today that's workshop-chatgpt/index.html). All six keep the same constraints: phone first, no external assets, works on a weak connection, nothing saved, IA Lab palette. They differ in layout, tone and density.

Each phone scrolls on its own. In option 6 the question works, so you can tap an answer.
